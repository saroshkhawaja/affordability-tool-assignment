How to read this: Purchasing power (GNI per capita, PPP) — higher means people there can afford more with their income. Price level — how expensive the country is compared to the US (1.0 = same as US). Inflation — how fast prices are rising. Lower price level and lower inflation, with higher purchasing power, mean better affordability.

Limitation: this reflects general cost of living, not exact tuition or rent for a specific university. A few countries with distorted exchange rates (e.g. sanctions-affected economies) may show an unusually high price level — treat those as outliers, not literal cost comparisons.
